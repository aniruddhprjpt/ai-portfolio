# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and senior developers at tech companies in the UK and globally. They open 10+ portfolios a day. Context: desktop browser, fast scroll, 20 seconds to decide if they read further. Secondary audience: other developers who might collaborate or refer.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Aniruddh Prajapati — CS graduate, full-stack and ML engineer. Goal: communicate technical depth and creative ability in under one scroll, then reward those who read further with evidence. Success: a recruiter saves the link and reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Energetic. Creative. Futuristic. Not a résumé with CSS — a developer who cares about craft. The work speaks first; the personality reinforces it. Confident without being loud.

## Anti-references

- Purple glassmorphism portfolios (blurry cards, violet blob backgrounds, the 2024–2025 AI default)
- Boring developer résumé (white bg, plain list, no visual identity)
- Over-animated chaos (every element bounces, scroll feels slow, motion is the point)
- Startup SaaS landing pages (metric tiles, gradient hero CTAs, social proof rows)

## Design Principles

1. **The work leads.** Projects are the product. Layout serves them, not the other way around.
2. **Controlled energy.** Motion is intentional and fast. One well-timed entrance beats ten scattered micro-interactions.
3. **Identity through color.** Amber/orange primary + cyan accent on near-black. Not purple. Not beige. Not blue.
4. **Left-aligned confidence.** No centered-everything hero. Asymmetric compositions signal a point of view.
5. **Restraint on density.** Dense enough to feel substantial, spacious enough to breathe. Every element earns its place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion via prefers-reduced-motion. Body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1. Interactive elements keyboard-navigable.
